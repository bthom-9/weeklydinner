# Reader favourites collection

Updated October 26, 2026. Weektable now includes **40 recipes**: its 12 original
starter recipes plus **28 source-attributed adaptations** listed below.

## Selection and ratings

Every added recipe had an original-source rating of at least **4.5 out of 5**
from at least **10 votes** when checked. The displayed score, vote count and date
refer to the original recipe on the linked website. They do not rate this
condensed adaptation, represent kitchen testing by Weektable, or update live.
The app displays both the score and vote count; a small perfect score is not
treated as stronger evidence than hundreds of ratings.

There are nine recipes from Amanda Biddle's Striped Spatula and nineteen from
Namiko Hirasawa Chen's Just One Cookbook. The ingredient quantities and cooking
parameters were checked against the linked recipe cards. Instructions were
independently condensed. No source photographs, introductory articles or reader
reviews are included.

## Shopping and serving choices

- Ingredient amounts scale from the documented base yield. Cooking duration,
  pan capacity and seasoning reduction do not necessarily scale linearly. Use
  batches where the method specifies them; the pan-fried curry chicken includes
  a specific caution about increasing sauce quantities.
- When the source offers a serving range, the adaptation declares the selected
  yield. Where an ingredient is given as a range, a documented value is used.
- Cooked rice is explicitly listed by cooked weight. Buy ready-cooked rice or
  prepare it separately. The app does not invent a raw-to-cooked conversion.
- Some recipes cover a main dish only. Omitted optional sides and garnishes are
  recorded in the adaptation notes; they are not silently added to shopping.
- Salt, pepper or other ingredients given only “to taste” remain flagged as
  unspecified. Measured quantities can be added together; unspecified amounts
  are not presented as exact totals.
- Whole eggs, cans, vegetable pieces and similar ingredients may scale to
  fractional quantities. The list shows the recipe requirement, not a rounded
  supermarket package purchase.
- Different ingredient states, package sizes and incompatible units remain
  separate. The collection supplies explicit aliases for synonymous names such
  as scallions and green onions without converting weight to volume.

The per-recipe attribution and adaptation notes remain in the app and backup.
[RECIPE_SOURCES.json](RECIPE_SOURCES.json) records the source URL, original yield,
chosen base yield, timing, score and vote count for every addition.

## Adding the collection to an existing installation

Export a full backup first. Open **My kitchen → Choose recipe files** and choose
`weektable-recipe-pack.json`, or the identical
`recipe-packs/reader-favourites.json` from this repository. This is a recipe
collection, so use the recipe importer rather than **Restore backup**.

A new installation of this repository already includes all 40 recipes. On an
existing installation, the updated app keeps the saved bundled recipe versions
and personal imports and appends newly shipped recipe IDs. Existing saved plans
continue to refer to their original recipe versions. Update the app code before importing to retain and display all source ratings
and notes. Earlier versions can still import ingredients and methods but may
discard the additional metadata.

## Attribution and content rights

The app code is MIT licensed. The twelve original Weektable recipes retain their
original CC0 dedication. These third-party adaptations are a separate collection
for personal, noncommercial use; they are not represented as public-domain or
openly licensed source content. Original authors retain their rights.

Striped Spatula's [sharing guidelines](https://stripedspatula.com/about/faq/)
allow adaptations with newly written instructions and a specific recipe link.
Just One Cookbook's [content policy](https://www.justonecookbook.com/terms-and-conditions/)
requires attribution and links and retains restrictions on its content. Each
record carries its source policy and credit. Keep those fields when exporting or
sharing; check the source's terms before commercial reuse or redistribution.

Some independently written methods add temperature checks using the
[FoodSafety.gov minimum-temperature chart](https://www.foodsafety.gov/food-safety-charts/safe-minimum-internal-temperatures).
The collection has been checked for software behavior and source consistency;
these adaptations have not been kitchen-tested for this project.
